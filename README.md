# packlist

Tell it the trip, the number of days, the weather and who's going. It makes a packing checklist with
quantities, grouped and ready to tick off.

Live: https://mridulsaravanan.com/packlist/

- **Seven kinds of trip:** city break, beach, hills or trek, work trip, visiting family, wedding, pilgrimage.
- **Quantities that add up:** clothes are counted per person for up to a week (one wash planned for longer
  trips), diapers at about 7 a day plus spares, and so on.
- **Sections for babies, kids and older travellers** only when they're coming, including things like lower
  berths on trains and wheelchair assistance at airports.
- **Abroad:** passports, visas, plug adapters, currency and a local SIM.
- Tick things off, add your own items, save lists, print, or copy the list as text for WhatsApp.
  Lists are saved on the device.

`engine.js` holds the packing rules. `index.html` is the page. Font: Pixelify Sans
(SIL Open Font License, `assets/OFL-PixelifySans.txt`).

Testing: a golden set of 10 trips (Goa, Manali in winter, a Mumbai work trip, a Jaipur wedding, Kerala in the
monsoon, a Tirupati pilgrimage, London, Dubai with a baby, a Himalayan trek, a weekend home) with items that must
and must not appear and exact quantities, 1,697 checks in all, plus 52 browser checks on desktop and phone.
