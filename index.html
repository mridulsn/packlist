      About packlist

      packlist turns a few details about your trip into a packing checklist, with quantities worked out from the number of days, the weather and who's coming.

      Clothes are counted for up to a week. Tick "I can do laundry there" and longer trips are planned around one wash.

      Tick things off as you pack, add your own, then save the list or copy it as text for WhatsApp. Saved lists stay on this device.

      It's a starting point, not a rulebook: check visa and airline rules for your own trip.
